Donations and contributions

Your contributions can make a significant difference in the lives of children who lack access to quality education.

Why give

Small things change a classroom.

Donating to Edugrow provides essential resources such as textbooks, school supplies, technology and educational tools that improve the learning experience for children in need.

Edugrow doesn't only raise money for schools. We also collect old electronic devices and extracurricular supplies and deliver them where they're needed most.
