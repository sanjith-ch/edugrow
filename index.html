What is Edugrow?

Everyone has hidden potential. Let's unlock it.

In government schools in India, many students never get the chance to discover what they're great at. We're working together to change that.

  • Few extracurriculars. Art, music, dance and sports are rarely taught, so talents go unnoticed.
  • No guest lectures. Students miss out on awareness of important topics beyond the syllabus.
  • Missing supplies. Books, stationery and devices are scarce, and infrastructure is worn down.

Everyone deserves a quality education and the chance to explore their interests. Edugrow gives people the platform to help, conveniently and directly.
