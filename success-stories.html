💛 Wellbeing
July 2026 Mental health with Project Wabi

Edugrow × Project Wabi

A mental health initiative

Edugrow teamed up with Project Wabi to bring a mental health initiative to government school students, giving children a safe, joyful space to express themselves and talk about how they feel.

Through hands-on creative activities, students made cards and painted giant handprint murals together, turning sheets of paper into a riot of colour. The day was full of laughter, paint-covered hands and plenty of smiles.

A big thank you to the Project Wabi team and our volunteers for helping put wellbeing at the heart of learning.

September 2026 Nekhnampur Government Primary School with FinTeen

Edugrow × FinTeen

Financial literacy for young learners

Edugrow partnered with FinTeen to teach financial literacy skills to students at Nekhnampur Government Primary School, helping children build money skills early that will stay with them for life.

The workshop went so well that a second FinTeen × Edugrow workshop followed in October 2026 at Zilla Parishad Government School.

🌲 Merry!
December 20, 2025 Nekhnampur School 146 students

A Very Merry Christmas Event 🌲

Edugrow celebrated the holiday season with 146 students from Grades 1 to 5 at Nekhnampur School. With the help of our wonderful volunteers, the school was filled with festive cheer as students took part in Christmas-themed activities.

From designing colourful ornaments to crafting paper snowflakes, the children showcased their creativity. The fun continued with an exciting round of "Pin the Ornament on the Christmas Tree," and by the end the trees were beautifully decorated with the students' creations. Gifts were distributed to every child, ending the celebration with smiles all around.

We're incredibly grateful to our volunteers for creating such a joyful experience and look forward to many more celebrations!

🎉 Summer!
April 19, 2025 Oakridge International School Grade 5

Last Day of School Event 🎉

What better way to begin the summer holidays than with a day full of fun, laughter, and learning? Edugrow's Last Day of School Event brought together 5th-grade students from government schools and volunteers at Oakridge International School for an unforgettable day of activities and connection.

Students explored new experiences, built friendships and showcased their talents, with activities ranging from exciting sports stations to creative art. The celebrations concluded with snacks, lunch and gift bags for every student.

A huge thank you to all our volunteers and partners. Together, we created memories that will last far beyond the final school bell!

🪔 Diwali
November 2, 2024 MPPS Kondapur

Diya Painting Workshop

Edugrow hosted a vibrant Diya Painting Workshop at MPPS Kondapur. With the support of our dedicated volunteers, we turned the Diwali season into an unforgettable experience, helping children unleash their creativity and paint festive diyas to celebrate the festival of lights.

Our enthusiastic volunteers guided and encouraged the kids throughout the session, and the children proudly took home their beautifully decorated diyas. A big thank you to everyone who helped make it possible!
