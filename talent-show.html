The Talent Show

In 2022, Edugrow organised an exceptional event at Oakridge International School, Gachibowli: a Talent Show that gave more than a hundred and fifty students from five government schools a platform to show their diverse abilities, capabilities and progress.

The event became a vibrant tapestry of creativity, with fascinating Goldilocks plays and spellbinding musical performances. It was not only a chance for students to exhibit their skills but also a moment of revelation, inspiring them to discover their potential further. Fun and practical prizes were given to every student who took part.
